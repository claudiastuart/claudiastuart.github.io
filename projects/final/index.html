Why are teenagers thrfiting?

Is it affordability, influence, uniqueness, or all of the above?

By Claudia Stuart

The art of thrifting has grown throughout generations, and it has become very popular and trendy in our current generation to thrift your clothes. There is a lot that goes into thrifting, it’s not all about just saving your money. People thrift to find unique pieces of furniture, decor, shoes, and even just get ideas for creating new outfits or even decorating their homes. The question of why it has become so popular now, when we have so many name brands that are equally as popular, is one to look at. Three main reasons to explore why everyone loves to thrift: affordability, influence, and uniqueness.
